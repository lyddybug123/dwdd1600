Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship is the responsible, respectful, and safe use of technology and the internet.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law refers to the rules and legal rights that govern how people use technology, including issues like copyright, privacy, and cybercrime.

What is AUP?

An AUP is a set of rules explaining how you are allowed to use a school or company's technology and internet resources.

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is using someone else's work or ideas without giving them proper credit.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty includes cheating, copying work, or lying about your work in an academic setting.

What are the Rules for AI Generated Content?

Rules for AI-generated content usually require you to use AI responsibly, not submit it as your own work without permission, and properly disclose or cite its use when required.
